# TiltPal Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026. This policy applies specifically to TiltPal, including the Android and iOS app with the identifier `com.posturepal.tiltpal`.

## Camera and posture processing

During a monitoring session, TiltPal uses the front camera to estimate body landmarks and compare posture changes with the reference you calibrate. Images and temporary landmark coordinates are processed on your phone. They are not recorded, saved as photos or video, uploaded to us, or shared with third parties. TiltPal does not identify people or create a facial recognition profile.

The posture model is bundled with the app. Monitoring does not require an account, a computer connection, or an internet connection. Switching away from TiltPal or locking the phone ends the session and stops camera monitoring.

## Motion and audio

Phone motion readings help compensate for phone orientation and detect changes in placement. These readings are used temporarily on the device and are not uploaded or retained in session history. TiltPal plays bundled reminder sounds or uses installed system speech voices. It does not request microphone access or record audio.

## Information stored on your phone

TiltPal stores your language and reminder preferences locally. It can also retain the last 30 completed monitoring records, containing a start time, reliable monitoring duration, and reminder count. These records contain no camera images, video, or body landmark coordinates. The current posture calibration is held in memory for the session and is not sent to us.

You can delete session records using **Settings / Sessions → Clear session history**. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data through the operating system. TiltPal does not provide a cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used during a session for local posture estimation.
- **Motion sensors:** used for phone orientation and placement checks.
- **Audio output:** used to play reminders through your selected speaker, headphones, or Bluetooth route.

TiltPal does not access your photo library, contacts, location, or microphone. You can revoke camera permission in your device settings; camera monitoring will then be unavailable.

## Advertising, analytics, purchases, and support

TiltPal does not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal data. App purchases, refunds, and platform services are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We do not receive your payment card details. Operating-system or app-store diagnostics, if enabled in your platform settings, are handled through those platforms.

If you choose to email support, the information you include in that email is used to respond to your request. Do not send camera footage or other sensitive information unless you choose to do so. TiltPal does not otherwise upload support information automatically.

## Children and changes

TiltPal has no account registration or automatic personal-data collection endpoint, including for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If this policy changes, its effective date and this page will be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact [blackjxx@gmail.com](mailto:blackjxx@gmail.com).

# TiltPal 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日。本政策仅适用于 TiltPal，以及应用标识为 `com.posturepal.tiltpal` 的 Android 和 iOS 应用。

## 摄像头和姿态处理

监测期间，TiltPal 使用前置摄像头估计人体关键点，并将姿态变化与您校准的个人基准比较。画面和临时关键点坐标仅在手机本机处理，不录制、不保存为照片或视频、不上传给我们，也不向第三方分享。TiltPal 不识别个人身份，不建立人脸识别档案。

姿态模型随应用提供。监测无需账号、电脑连接或网络连接。切换应用或锁屏会结束监测并停止使用摄像头。

## 运动传感器和声音

手机运动数据用于补偿手机方向并检查摆放位置变化，仅在本机临时使用，不上传，也不保存在监测记录中。提醒使用应用内置提示音或已安装的系统语音。TiltPal 不申请麦克风权限，不录音。

## 本机保存的信息

TiltPal 在本机保存语言和提醒偏好，以及最近 30 次已完成的监测记录，包括开始时间、有效监测时长和提醒次数。记录不含摄像头画面、视频或人体关键点坐标。当前姿态校准仅在本次监测的内存中使用，不发送给我们。

您可以在“记录”页面使用“清除监测记录”删除记录。卸载应用后，操作系统会移除应用的本地数据。TiltPal 不提供云端账号或云同步服务。

## 权限

- 摄像头：仅用于监测期间在本机估计姿态。
- 运动传感器：用于补偿方向和检查手机位置变化。
- 声音输出：通过所选扬声器、耳机或蓝牙设备播放提醒。

TiltPal 不访问相册、通讯录、位置或麦克风。您可以在系统设置中撤销摄像头权限，撤销后无法使用摄像头监测。

## 广告、统计、购买与支持

TiltPal 不集成广告或统计分析 SDK，不出售个人数据。购买、退款及平台服务由 Apple 或 Google 按其各自政策处理；我们不会收到您的银行卡信息。如您在系统或商店设置中开启诊断，相关诊断由对应平台处理。

如您主动发送支持邮件，邮件中提供的信息仅用于回复和处理您的请求。请自主决定是否发送画面或其他敏感信息。应用不会自动上传支持信息。

## 儿童与政策变更

TiltPal 没有账号注册或自动收集个人数据的服务接口，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。政策变更时，我们会更新本页及生效日期。
